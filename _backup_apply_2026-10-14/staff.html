    Semester Registration

    Students register for their next semester from their portal (new students start in Semester I). A registration is approved in order by the Accounts office (minimum fees paid), Class Advisor, Head of Department, Dean of Students and Dean (Academic) — the last approval moves the student up to that semester.

    Direct entry & exemptions

    Exempt a student from semesters they don't have to take — for example Semesters I–II for a direct-entry student who already holds a diploma. A new direct-entry student starts at the first semester they are not exempted from, and registration always skips exempted semesters.
